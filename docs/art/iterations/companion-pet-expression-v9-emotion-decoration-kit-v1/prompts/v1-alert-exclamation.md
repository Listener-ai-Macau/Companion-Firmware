# V35 Alert Exclamation Decoration Source, Revision 1

Use the attached locked V9 Companion face only as the style reference. Generate
an art-source sheet for a tiny three-beat alert-exclamation decoration component,
not a character portrait.

Pure white background. Pure black, crisp monochrome, small rounded V9 line
grammar. Arrange exactly three clearly separated, equally sized poses from left
to right with generous blank gaps and no separators. Every pose is one compact,
friendly rounded exclamation mark designed to hover above-center of an unseen
V9 face. Beat 1 is a short quiet exclamation mark, beat 2 has the stem rise
slightly before the dot, beat 3 is the clear full mark with a tiny restrained
settle. It must read as alert attention, not danger, anger or a UI warning.

Do not draw a face, eyes, eyebrows, mouth, cheeks, body, hands, ears, speech
bubble, text other than the single exclamation-mark symbol, label, grid, frame,
border, menu UI, color, gray, shadow, texture, lightning bolt or battery icon.

This is an animation-component source only. It will later be cropped into three
registered masks and composed above the unchanged locked V9 daily face.
