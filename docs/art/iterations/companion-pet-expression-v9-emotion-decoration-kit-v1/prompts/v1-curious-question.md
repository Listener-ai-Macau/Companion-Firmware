# V35 Curious Question Decoration Source, Revision 1

Use the attached locked V9 Companion face only as the style reference. Generate
an art-source sheet for a tiny three-beat curious-question decoration component,
not a character portrait.

Pure white background. Pure black, crisp monochrome, small rounded V9 line
grammar. Arrange exactly three clearly separated, equally sized poses from left
to right with generous blank gaps and no separators. Every pose is one small,
friendly rounded question mark designed to hover above-right of an unseen V9
face. Beat 1 is a compact resting question mark, beat 2 lifts the curved hook
slightly as if a thought arrives, beat 3 settles one pixel-like step higher with
its small dot following last. Keep it simple, light and expressive at 200x200.

Do not draw a face, eyes, eyebrows, mouth, cheeks, body, hands, ears, speech
bubble, text other than the single question-mark symbol, label, grid, frame,
border, menu UI, color, gray, shadow or texture.

This is an animation-component source only. It will later be cropped into three
registered masks and composed above the unchanged locked V9 daily face.
