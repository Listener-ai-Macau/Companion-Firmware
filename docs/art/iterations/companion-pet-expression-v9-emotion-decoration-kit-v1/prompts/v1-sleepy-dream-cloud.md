# V35 Sleepy Dream Cloud Decoration Source, Revision 1

Use the attached locked V9 Companion face only as the style reference. Generate
an art-source sheet for a tiny three-beat sleepy-dream-cloud decoration component,
not a character portrait.

Pure white background. Pure black, crisp monochrome, small rounded V9 line
grammar. Arrange exactly three clearly separated, equally sized poses from left
to right with generous blank gaps and no separators. Every pose is one small
puffy dream cloud with two or three rounded lobes, designed to float above-left
of an unseen V9 face. Beat 1 is a compact quiet cloud, beat 2 drifts gently
up-left with one tiny trailing puff, beat 3 drifts a little farther while the
trailing puff follows. It must read as sleepy dreaming, not weather, smoke,
anger or a speech bubble.

Do not draw a face, eyes, eyebrows, mouth, cheeks, body, hands, ears, a speech
bubble, text, letters, Z symbols, label, grid, frame, border, menu UI, color,
gray, shadow or texture.

This is an animation-component source only. It will later be cropped into three
registered masks and composed above the unchanged locked V9 daily face.
