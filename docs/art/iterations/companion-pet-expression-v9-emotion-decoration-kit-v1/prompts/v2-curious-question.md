# V35 Curious Question Decoration Source, Revision 2

Use the attached locked V9 Companion face only as the style reference. Generate
an art-source sheet for a three-beat curious-question decoration component, not
a character portrait and not a UI icon set.

Pure white background. Pure black crisp monochrome. Make exactly three clearly
separated, equally scaled poses from left to right, each one a small friendly
question mark for an unseen V9 face. The mark must have a soft rounded hook, a
short compact lower tail and one detached rounded dot. The hook should feel
hand-drawn in the same light, clean V9 line grammar, but the ink must remain
thick enough to survive a 26 by 30 pixel target box. Pose one arrives small and
low, pose two lifts the hook with the dot delayed, and pose three settles one
tiny step higher. Keep the mark narrow, quiet and readable, not oversized.

Do not draw a face, eyes, eyebrows, mouth, cheeks, body, hands, ears, speech
bubble, text other than the single question-mark symbol, label, grid, frame,
border, menu UI, sparkle, star, color, gray, shadow or texture.

This is an animation-component source only. It will later be cropped into three
registered masks and composed above the unchanged locked V9 daily face.
