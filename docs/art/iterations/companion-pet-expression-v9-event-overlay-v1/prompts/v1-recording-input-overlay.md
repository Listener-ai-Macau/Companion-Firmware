# V34 Recording-Input Overlay Source, Revision 1

Use the attached locked V9 Companion face only as the style reference. Generate
an art-source sheet for a tiny three-beat recording-input animation component,
not a character portrait.

Pure white background. Pure black, crisp monochrome, small rounded V9 line
grammar. Arrange exactly three clearly separated, equally sized component poses
left to right with generous blank gaps and no separators. Every pose contains a
small, simple vertical listening microphone/sensor capsule on the outer right
and one or two short rounded sound arcs placed to its left. The arcs imply quiet
incoming sound travelling from the environment/microphone toward an unseen
character on the left: beat 1 is calm idle, beat 2 has one inward pulse, beat 3
has a second tighter inward pulse. Keep all marks compact, friendly and
minimal, designed to sit beside a face at 200x200 rather than dominate it.

Do not draw a face, eyes, eyebrows, mouth, cheeks, body, hands, ears, speech
bubble, outward sound, waveform, text, labels, grid, frame, border, menu UI,
headset, cable, battery, lightning bolt, color, gray, shadow or texture.

This is an animation-component source only. It will later be cropped into three
registered masks and composed beside the unchanged locked V9 daily face.
