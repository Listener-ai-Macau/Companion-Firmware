# V34 Wireless-Charging Overlay Source, Revision 1

Use the attached locked V9 Companion face only as the style reference. Generate
an art-source sheet for a tiny three-beat wireless-charging animation component,
not a character portrait.

Pure white background. Pure black, crisp monochrome, small rounded V9 line
grammar. Arrange exactly three clearly separated, equally sized component poses
left to right with generous blank gaps and no separators. Every pose contains a
compact low U-shaped wireless charging cradle/dock, viewed straight on, broad
enough to support an unseen round companion face above it. Beat 1 is the quiet
empty cradle silhouette; beat 2 adds one short rounded induction arc rising
straight upward from its center; beat 3 adds two small nested upward induction
arcs. Keep it modest, friendly and physical, designed to sit beneath a face at
200x200 rather than become a large icon.

Do not draw a face, eyes, eyebrows, mouth, cheeks, body, hands, ears, plug,
cable, socket, Type-C connector, battery icon, lightning bolt, text, labels,
grid, frame, border, menu UI, color, gray, shadow or texture.

This is an animation-component source only. It will later be cropped into three
registered masks and composed beneath the unchanged locked V9 daily face.
