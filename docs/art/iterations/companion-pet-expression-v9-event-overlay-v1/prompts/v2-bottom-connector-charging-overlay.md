# V34 Bottom-Connector Charging Overlay Source, Revision 2

Use the attached locked V9 Companion face only as the style reference. Generate
an art-source sheet for a tiny three-beat bottom-connector charging animation
component, not a character portrait.

Pure white background. Pure black, crisp monochrome, small rounded V9 line
grammar. Arrange exactly three clearly separated, equally sized component poses
left to right with generous blank gaps and no separators. Every pose shows a
compact shallow underside connector/charging plug seated from below into the
bottom center of an unseen companion. Beat 1 is the quiet seated bottom
connector; beat 2 adds one tiny restrained rounded energy pulse rising from
the connector; beat 3 adds two tiny nested upward pulses. It must read as a
real attachment at the character's underside, not a detached charging dock.
Keep all marks friendly, minimal and compact enough to fit below a 200x200 face.

Do not draw a face, eyes, eyebrows, mouth, cheeks, body, hands, ears, a
dangling cable, plug lead, battery icon, lightning bolt, text, labels, grid,
frame, border, menu UI, color, gray, shadow or texture.

This is an animation-component source only. It will later be cropped into three
registered masks and composed beneath the unchanged locked V9 daily face.
