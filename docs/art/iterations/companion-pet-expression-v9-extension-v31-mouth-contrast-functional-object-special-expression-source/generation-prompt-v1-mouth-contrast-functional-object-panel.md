## Shared Rules

The face is primary. Each state has exactly three small separately extractable
black object components, each smaller than half one V9 eye. Objects are compact
character equipment, not detached UI icons. Do not use battery, lightning,
microphone, waveform, plug, text, labels or large props.

Each state must have a natural, visibly different and separately extractable
mouth contour. The three mouths must not be the same shape shifted by a pixel.
Avoid a surprised open O and avoid exaggerated expressions.

## Column 1: Menu Active

Low composed brows, gaze steady. Use one compact shallow closed decision mouth
with a tiny deliberate right-corner lift; this is not the V9 double-wave mouth.
Above the brows add exactly three rounded page tabs with clear white gaps. The
centre tab is subtly higher, and the tabs are wide enough to read as a compact
navigation strip rather than dots or hair. No large hat or menu glyph.

## Column 2: Recording Active

Receptive brows and an attentive gaze. Use one tiny calm listening mouth made
from a soft shallow U-shaped contour, not an open O or surprise face. Add two
thin but clearly readable C-shaped ear-cuffs at the outer cheeks, plus one very
short rounded pickup capsule beside the right mouth corner. No headband, full
earcups, long boom, microphone pictogram or waveform.

## Column 3: Charging Active

Soft released brows and a recovering gaze. Use one short relaxed upward recovery
smile made from a single smooth contour, clearly unlike the menu and recording
mouths. Add a compact open side clasp beside the left cheek with a small white
inner notch, one very short curved tether ending below the brow, and a small
rounded four-point energy core above the brows. The connection should be readable
at native 200x200 without a big plug, battery or lightning glyph.
