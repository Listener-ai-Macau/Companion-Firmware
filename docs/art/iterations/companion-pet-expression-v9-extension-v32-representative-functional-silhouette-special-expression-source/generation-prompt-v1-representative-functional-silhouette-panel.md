## Shared Rules

The face is primary. Each state has exactly three small separately extractable
black functional components, each smaller than half one V9 eye. Components are
character-bound line art and must not cover the eyes or brows. Do not use a
battery, lightning bolt, microphone, waveform, literal plug, generic menu icon,
text, label or detached UI widget.

Each state must have a natural separately extractable mouth with a strongly
different ink shape. The three mouths must not share one mask, or differ only by
offset or scale. At 200x200, the three faces must read as navigation, active
listening/recording and connected charging without captions.

## Column 1: Menu Active

Use steady low composed brows and a short confident straight decision mouth.
The mouth has two clear horizontal endpoints and one small centred downward
selection notch, not a smile, U, O or V9 double-wave mouth. Above the brows add
exactly three separated stepped page tabs: a left rounded tab, a clearly raised
centre active tab with one tiny white folded-corner notch, and a right rounded
tab. Their crisp white gaps must make them read as tiny layered pages rather
than hair, dots or a crown. No big hat and no menu glyph.

## Column 2: Recording Active

Use receptive attentive brows. Add a short shallow rounded horizontal listening
capsule mouth below the nose: flatter and wider than a U, with rounded ends and
a tiny white inner opening. It is not an O, surprise mouth or smile. Add exactly
two open thin receiver cuffs immediately outside the cheeks. Each cuff is a
C-shaped outer arc with one compact inward contact tick. Add one small rounded
pickup capsule beside the mouth. There is no head bridge, no filled earcup, no
long boom, no microphone pictogram or waveform.

## Column 3: Charging Active

Use soft released brows and a small smooth upward recovery smile with gently
lifted endpoints and an open white interior. It must differ clearly from both
the menu straight-notch mouth and recording flat capsule. Add exactly three
connected power-route parts: an open side coupler beside the left cheek with a
white inner contact notch, one short visible tether that runs from the coupler,
and a small rounded energy dock above the brows with a horizontal white inset
contact slot. The tether must visibly join coupler to dock so the three forms
read as a compact connected charging action, never a decorative star on a
string. No battery, lightning or oversized charge object.
