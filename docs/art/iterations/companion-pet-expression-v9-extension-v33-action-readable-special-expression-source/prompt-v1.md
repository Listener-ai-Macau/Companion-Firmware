# V33 Action-Readable Special Expression Panel

Use only the supplied locked V9 reference image. Create one clean, black on
white, horizontal three-column source panel with no labels, captions, borders,
cards, text, colour, shading or grey. Each column contains the same floating V9
face with wide white margins. The left column is Menu, the middle is Recording,
and the right is Charging, but do not print those names.

Keep the exact V9 identity: two black oval eyes with one oval highlight and one
diamond sparkle in each eye, the same eye size, spacing and baseline, the small
nose, cheek dots, thin black line weight and empty white field. Do not draw a
head outline, hair, ears, body, helmet, headband or a whole-face prop.

The face is primary: brows, mouth and highlight direction must visibly act
before the viewer reads the compact functional cue. Every cue is attached to
the character's face field and is designed for a 200x200 monochrome display.
Do not make detached UI cards, panels, text or labels. Keep clean white gaps so
eyes, brows, mouth, cheeks and action cues can later be separated as individual
component masks. The three states need strongly different eyebrow poses, mouth
silhouettes, highlight directions and action verbs.

Left face: the character is clearly BROWSING AND CHOOSING a menu. Give it
focused level brows with one eyebrow slightly raised, highlights looking toward
the active choice, and a short decisive horizontal mouth with one tiny centered
selection notch. Directly above the brows, draw a compact unmistakable
three-row menu mark: three short horizontal lines with one row visibly selected
by one tiny adjacent cursor wedge. It must read as a menu choice, not paper
tabs, hair, a crown, random dots, a question mark or a detached card.

Middle face: the character is clearly SPEAKING AND LISTENING while recording.
Give it attentive gently drawn-in brows, highlights aimed toward the sound
source, and one small rounded speaking mouth just open below the nose. Beside
that mouth, draw one familiar tiny classic microphone and exactly two very short
sound arcs travelling from the mouth toward the microphone. Keep this calm and
compact: no costume headset, no ear cuffs, no boom, no moustache, no large
waveform, no duplicate mouth and no surprised O expression.

Right face: the character is clearly PLUGGED IN AND CHARGING. Give it soft
relaxed brows, highlights lifted upward and a compact restorative smile rather
than a generic celebration. At the left cheek, draw one familiar tiny two-prong
plug. A single short continuous cord visibly connects that plug to a small
clean lightning-energy mark above the brow. The plug, cord and bolt must touch
as one unmistakable charging action, never a loose string, decorative star,
battery block, oversized plug or a merely happy daily face.
