one unlabelled square daily-down source face. Preserve the V9 black-and-white

black open-eye outer silhouettes, eye positions, cheeks, nose and mouth. Do

reflection in the lower-inner area of each eye. It must be smaller than the
reference's dominant upper reflection and must NOT be a large circular white
disc. Place one clearly smaller sparkle nearby, toward the nose side of its
primary reflection, so each eye has one compact reflection group. Leave a
generous continuous black rim above and below every white mark. The two eyes
must be balanced and symmetric. Avoid scattered white holes, detached diagonal
sparkles, cross-eye asymmetry, surprise, and any white mark touching an eye
edge. The outer black eye shape, eye scale and placement must remain the same
as the locked V9 reference.
