# V42 Emotion Source Prompt

Using the attached locked V9 sheet as the sole character reference, generate
one square white-background monochrome source-art sheet containing exactly four
complete centered V9 Companion faces in an unlabeled 2 by 2 grid with generous
white separation. This is source art for later component extraction, never a
runtime face-card sheet.

Every face must keep the exact V9 identity: two open rounded black eye bodies,
the same eye scale and spacing, one white primary highlight and one tiny white
sparkle in each eye, and the small centered nose anchor. Do not vary the eye
shape. Make the four poses visibly distinct chiefly through brows, mouth, and
cheeks:

1. Delighted: both brows visibly lifted and open; a wider rounded upturned
   smile; higher fuller cheeks.
2. Bashful: soft brows gently converging inward; a small compact shy smile;
   visibly closer rounded cheeks; cute and quiet, not worried.
3. Thoughtful: one brow distinctly higher, the other relaxed; a small
   off-center thinking mouth; one subtly lifted cheek; no punctuation.
4. Focused: low gently inward brows; a short confident compact mouth; calm low
   cheeks; attentive and determined but never angry.

No labels, captions, borders, cards, device frame, UI, text, grid lines,
pupils, eyelashes, eyelids, wink, crying, human or animal face, color, gray,
antialiasing, texture, face outline, detached punctuation, functional status
symbols, moon, cloud, legacy art, whole-face runtime playback, custom LUT, or
code-drawn geometry.
