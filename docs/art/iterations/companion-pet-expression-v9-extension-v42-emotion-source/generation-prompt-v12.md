# V42 Delighted Mouth Source Prompt V12

Use the attached locked V9 sheet as the sole visual reference. Generate one
pure-white square source sheet showing the same V9 Companion face. This image
is only a source sheet for extracting component 577, the Delighted mouth; it
is never a whole-face runtime animation card.

Keep the exact V9 open eyes, eye bodies, one primary white highlight and one
tiny sparkle per eye, brows, cheeks, centered rounded nose, spacing, scale and
black monochrome ink weight. Change only the mouth.

Make the mouth small, cute, level and perfectly centered on the V9 nose axis.
It should be closer to the V9 normal mouth scale than a grin: a short smooth
upper curve, a shallow lower curve, a tiny centered white opening, mirrored
left and right contour mass, and rounded ends. At native 200 by 200 the mouth
must be 22 to 26 pixels wide and 8 to 12 pixels high, with its bbox center
within one pixel of the nose centerline. The left and right sides must be
visually and pixel-wise balanced.

Do not draw a wide U, wide O, bowl, oversized grin, surprised mouth, thick
filled shape, diagonal smile, uneven end, thin disconnected arcs, teeth,
tongue, W-mouth, moustache, frown, or extra mouth line. Do not move the nose,
eyes, brows or cheeks.

No labels, captions, border, card, UI, text, grid, new eye shape, new nose
shape, nostrils, pupils, eyelashes, eyelids, wink, crying, color, gray,
antialiasing, texture, legacy art, whole-face playback, custom LUT, or
code-drawn geometry.
