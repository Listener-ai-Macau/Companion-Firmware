# V42 Emotion Source Prompt V2

Using the attached locked V9 sheet as the sole character reference, generate
one square white-background monochrome source-art sheet containing exactly four
complete centered V9 Companion faces in an unlabeled 2 by 2 grid with generous
white separation. This is source art for later component extraction only,
never a runtime face-card sheet.

Every face must retain the exact V9 open black eye body, eye scale, eye spacing,
one white primary highlight and one tiny white sparkle in each eye, and the
centered V9 nose anchor. Do not vary the eye shape. The four faces must remain
clearly distinct at native 200 by 200 scale through brow pair, mouth silhouette,
and cheek placement:

1. Delighted: two high open brow arcs; a clearly wider open U-smile with white
   interior; high full cheeks.
2. Bashful: two short gentle inward-softened brow arcs; a tiny closed
   asymmetric shy smile, never the default W-mouth; two compact cheeks visibly
   closer to the mouth.
3. Thoughtful: left high brow arch and right low relaxed brow arc; a small
   closed offset three-part pondering curve, never an O-mouth or surprise
   mouth; left cheek subtly higher than right.
4. Focused: two short low inward-sloping but rounded brows; a compact
   almost-flat confident mouth line with soft round ends, never a W-mouth or
   frown; two low even compact cheeks.

No labels, captions, borders, cards, device frame, UI, text, grid lines,
pupils, eyelashes, eyelids, wink, crying, human or animal face, color, gray,
antialiasing, texture, face outline, detached punctuation, functional status
symbols, moon, cloud, legacy art, whole-face runtime playback, custom LUT, or
code-drawn geometry.
