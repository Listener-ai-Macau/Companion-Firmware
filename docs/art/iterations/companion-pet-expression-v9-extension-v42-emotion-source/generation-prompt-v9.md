complete centered V9 Companion source pose on a pure white square sheet. This
is source art for extracting the Delighted mouth mask only, never a runtime
whole-face card.

Keep the exact V9 open eye bodies, eye scale and spacing, one white primary
highlight, one tiny white sparkle, centered small rounded nose, brows, cheeks,

space between the inner eye edges, must not approach the cheek width, and must

the ink weight close to the V9 nose and normal mouth.

gray, antialiasing, texture, legacy art, whole-face runtime playback, custom
