# V9 Functional Decoration Source v2

Create one square, high-resolution **black ink on pure white** source sheet for extracting tiny 1-bit e-paper component masks. Use the attached locked V9 character sheet only for its friendly rounded black line weight, sparse cute restraint, and clean white space. **Do not draw the character or any facial feature.**

Arrange exactly twelve isolated, unlabeled icon cells in a strict **3 columns x 4 rows** grid with generous blank gutters and no borders. Each cell contains only one small black functional ornament, centered in its cell and surrounded by white. No text, digits, letters, labels, panels, buttons, interface frames, shadows, gray, gradients, color, or decorative background.

Reading left to right, then top to bottom:

1. `recording_input_0`, `recording_input_1`, `recording_input_2`: a tiny upright microphone with very small curved sound marks moving **toward the microphone** to communicate input/listening, not output/broadcast. The three frames should grow by a gentle pulse and remain compact enough to sit beside a face.
2. `bottom_charge_0`, `bottom_charge_1`, `bottom_charge_2`: a tiny physical charging dock/plug motif designed to sit under the face near the bottom edge. Make it clearly read as a bottom connector or small wireless dock with a soft upward energy pulse. No lightning bolt and no cable crossing the face.
3. `upgrade_0`, `upgrade_1`, `upgrade_2`: a small clean upward arrow with a minimal dot/progress step that rises gently. It must be readable at 200x200 but not resemble a menu button.
4. `error_0`, `error_1`, `error_2`: a compact rounded caution diamond with a centered dot, appearing with a restrained little bounce. It should be clear but not alarming.

Keep all marks compatible with the V9 line thickness and use simple closed black strokes suitable for thresholding. The output is a component-source sheet, not a character-expression sheet and not a complete runtime frame.
